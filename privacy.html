Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers apps published by Mom and Pop Labs LLC.

What we collect

Very little. Our apps have no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers. We do not sell or share personal information.

Puzzle tallies in 84-0

The daily and weekly puzzles in 84-0 show how common each answer was. When you finish one, the app sends the players you picked and a random identifier created for that install, so each install counts once. The identifier is not connected to your name, your Apple Account, or any device identifier, and is used for nothing else. We keep the counts and the identifier on servers operated for us by Cloudflare. Deleting the app discards the identifier; a reinstall creates a new one.

What stays on your device

Game progress, scores, streaks, and preferences are saved on your device and go where your device backups go. Deleting the app deletes them.

Services run by Apple

  • Game Center. Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center if you are signed in to it. Scores you post are held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple Account details.
  • Apple Music. Song links open Apple Music. Where an app plays song previews, they stream from Apple.

Purchases

If an app offers purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment details.

Children

Our apps do not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future app or feature changes any of the above, this page will say so before that feature ships, with a new effective date.
