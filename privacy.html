Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

This policy covers apps published by Mom and Pop Labs LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps have no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no trackers. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Game progress, scores, streaks, and preferences are saved on your device and go where your device backups go. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests the apps make

  • Music previews. When an app plays soundtrack previews, it streams them from Apple's public iTunes/Apple Music services. Those requests go to Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy; we operate no server and receive nothing.

Our apps make no other network requests.

Purchases

If an app offers purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment details.

Children

Our apps do not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future app or feature changes any of the above, this page will say so before that feature ships, with a new effective date.
