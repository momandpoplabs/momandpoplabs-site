Mom and Pop Labs

Mom and Pop Labs is an independent software company. We design, build, and support our own apps for iPhone.

It's a small operation and we like it that way. No outside funding, no client work, no growth team. We make things we want to use, ship them when they're ready, and answer our own support email.

How we build

A few rules we hold ourselves to across everything we release: our apps work without an account. They work offline wherever that's possible. We don't sell or share user data — most of the time we never receive any. And if something breaks, the person reading your email is the person who can fix it.
