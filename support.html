Support

Email support@momandpoplabs.com. We reply within two business days, usually faster. Before you write, the answers below cover most of what lands in our inbox.

Billing and refunds

Purchases in our apps go through Apple, not us — we never see your payment details and we can't issue refunds ourselves. To request a refund, use Apple's form at reportaproblem.apple.com and Apple handles it directly, usually within a couple of days.

Restoring a purchase

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not your device. If something you bought isn't showing up, look for a Restore Purchases option in the app, and make sure you're signed in with the account that bought it. Still wrong? Email us.

Your data

Our apps keep progress and settings on your device. Deleting an app deletes its data, and iCloud device backups carry it between phones the same way they carry everything else. The privacy policy has the full picture — it's short, because we collect nothing.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what happened, what you expected instead, and your iPhone model and iOS version. A screenshot or screen recording saves a round trip. Bugs that we can reproduce get fixed first.

Feature requests

Send them to the same address. We read all of it and we don't promise timelines.
